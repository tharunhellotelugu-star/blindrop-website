What stays private

Your phone number is used only to verify your account (via Truecaller or Firebase phone verification) — it's never shown to anyone you match with. Your photo and name stay hidden through every conversation and only unlock if both people involved choose to reveal. Your city is used to schedule your daily Drop; your exact location never is.

Safety that's actually enforced

Every message is screened by automated AI moderation for harassment, hate speech, contact-info sharing, and spam before it's ever fully sent. Report or block anyone with a single tap, and once blocked, that person can never be matched with you again — enforced at the database level, not just a setting you toggle.

Frequently asked questions

How does Blindrop keep dating anonymous in India?

Blindrop never shows your phone number, photo, or name to a match. Sign-in verification happens through Truecaller or a private phone verification, but your number stays visible only to you. Matches only see who you are if you both choose to reveal after a conversation.

Is it safe to use an anonymous dating app in India?

Blindrop screens every conversation with automated AI moderation for harassment, hate speech, and spam, and lets you report or block anyone with one tap — blocked users are never matched with you again.

Does Blindrop show my city or exact location?

Blindrop matches you with people in your city for same-day Drops, but your precise location is never shared with a match — only your general city, the same way it's used to schedule that day's Drop.
