Child Safety Standards

Last updated: August 18, 2026

Blindrop has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) in any form. Blindrop is restricted to adults 18 years of age and older, and we take active steps to prevent minors from using the platform and to detect and report any attempt to use it to harm children.

1. Age restriction and eligibility

Blindrop's Terms of Service require every user to confirm they are 18 or older before creating an account. We do not knowingly permit anyone under 18 to register or use the app, and we act immediately to remove any account we identify as belonging to a minor.

2. Prevention and detection

  • Every message exchanged in a live Drop is screened by automated AI moderation for harassment, exploitation, hate speech, and other policy violations before being delivered.
  • Users can report or block any other user at any time, directly from within a conversation, with a single tap.
  • Accounts found to violate these standards are permanently banned, and the underlying report is preserved for law enforcement cooperation even if the reported account is later deleted.

3. Reporting CSAE content or behavior

If you encounter content or behavior on Blindrop that you believe involves child sexual abuse or exploitation, report it immediately using the in-app Report button, or contact us directly using the details below. We review and act on every CSAE report as our highest priority, and cooperate fully with law enforcement and the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and equivalent bodies where required.
