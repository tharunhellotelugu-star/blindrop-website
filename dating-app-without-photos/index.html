Frequently asked questions

Why would I use a dating app without photos?

Photos-first dating apps encourage snap judgments based on looks before you know anything else about someone. A dating app without photos, like Blindrop, forces a real conversation first, so first impressions come from personality and conversation instead of a picture.

Do photos ever appear on Blindrop?

Yes, but only after a conversation ends and both people independently choose to reveal. If either person passes, photos and names stay hidden and the match disappears entirely.
