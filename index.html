No browsing, no algorithmic feed to manage. Blindrop happens to you once a day — and it's over before it gets exhausting.

THE MUTUAL REVEAL GATE

Nothing to fake, because there's nothing to see yet.

Your photo and name stay hidden for the entire conversation. If you both tap Reveal, the blur lifts over 1.5 seconds and the chat keeps going — permanently. If either of you passes, the whole session disappears. No seen receipts. No record. No sting.

Built for one honest conversation, not infinite scroll.

AFTER EVERY DROP

A Vibe Report, win or lose.

Every conversation ends with a chemistry score, a one-line roast or compliment, and one real tip for next time — rendered as a clean card with zero personal data on it. Screenshot it, post it, or keep it to yourself.

It's the one part of Blindrop built to leave the app — sized for Stories, small mark in the corner, nothing else.
