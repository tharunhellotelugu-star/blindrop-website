Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 17, 2026

Blindrop ("we", "us", "our") operates the Blindrop mobile app, an anonymous voice & video dating app. This Privacy Policy explains what we collect, why, and how you can control or delete it.

1. Information We Collect

  • Phone number — verified during sign-up via Truecaller or Firebase Phone Auth (SMS), used solely to create and secure your account. It is never shown to other users.
  • Profile information — first name, age, gender, dating preference, city, and an optional profile photo.
  • Camera, for identity verification only — when you verify your account, we run an on-device face and blink liveness check. This confirms you're a real, present person; it does not store or upload the camera feed.
  • Chat messages — sent during a live Drop conversation. Messages are automatically screened by an AI moderation system for harassment, hate speech, spam, or attempts to share contact information, and may be redacted if flagged.
  • Voice & video call data — calls are routed through a real-time calling provider (Daily.co) and are not recorded or stored by Blindrop.
  • Usage & device data — crash logs and diagnostics (via Firebase Crashlytics) and a push-notification token (via Firebase Cloud Messaging), used to keep the app stable and notify you of matches.
  • Referral data — if you use or share an invite code, we record which accounts are linked for reward purposes.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and authenticate your account.
  • To match you with compatible people in scheduled "Drops."
  • To generate your AI-powered post-chat Vibe Report (a compatibility score and tip, generated using Google's Gemini AI based on your conversation).
  • To detect and remove abusive behavior and keep the community safe.
  • To show ads (via Google AdMob) that help keep Blindrop free.
  • To fix bugs and improve reliability.

3. Who We Share Data With

We do not sell your personal data. We share the minimum data necessary with service providers who process it on our behalf, under their own data-processing agreements:

  • Supabase (database, authentication, file storage)
  • Google Firebase (push notifications, crash reporting), Google AdMob (ads), Google Gemini (AI vibe scoring & moderation)
  • Truecaller (phone verification)
  • Daily.co (real-time voice/video calling)

Your identity is never revealed to a match unless both of you explicitly choose to reveal it after a Drop.

4. Your Choices

  • You can edit your profile at any time from within the app.
  • You can report or block anyone you match with.
  • You can permanently delete your account and associated data from Settings → Delete Account, or via our data removal page.

5. Data Retention

We keep your data only as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, your chat history, call sessions, and matches are permanently erased; a minimal record is retained only where needed to enforce safety bans, with your identifying details removed.

6. Children's Privacy

Blindrop is for users 18 years of age and older. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Material changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
